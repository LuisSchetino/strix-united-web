Who we are

Strix United is a competitive EA Sports FC club from Brazil. We built and run our own team platform at strixunited.com.br: match schedule, results, competitions, roster management, Discord-based member access and automated match media (recaps, announcements and highlight videos).

The team platform is private to club members, so this page describes what we do publicly.

What we're building: Next Clubs

Next Clubs is a platform to organize and run online EA Sports FC tournaments for clubs and players across Brazil, built on everything we learned running our own club.

RegistrationsClubs and players sign up for competitions in one place.
Fixtures and bracketsLeagues and knockouts generated and kept up to date.
Results and standingsScores, tables and stats for every competition.
Multiple competitionsSeveral tournaments running at once, then other competitive games.

See Next Clubs (in development)

How we use AI

We use Claude to automate tournament operations:

  • Writing match recaps, news and social posts from results
  • Generating fixtures and plain-language rule summaries
  • A Discord assistant that answers players' and organizers' questions
